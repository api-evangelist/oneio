Most enterprise IT leaders underestimate the integration costs of AI development. The standard guidance is to multiply the vendor quote by three or four, allocate a share of the budget to data preparation and integration engineering, and hold a contingency buffer. That gets first-year totals into a realistic range and still leaves the largest integration cost outside the model.
