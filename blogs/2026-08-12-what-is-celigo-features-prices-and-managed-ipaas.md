Celigo is an iPaaS built around prebuilt integration apps, strongest in ecommerce and NetSuite-centric estates. It launched Ora and Agent Builder in March 2026. Alternatives split into self-service iPaaS platforms you operate, and managed iPaaS such as ONEiO.
